# Code of conduct

This is a small, private-intended repository. Everyone working in it is
expected to:

- Treat colleagues, operators and reporters with respect. Disagree with the
  work, not the person.
- Say what you measured and on which branch, and report what happened —
  including when it is worse than expected. A number somebody assumed is
  worth less than a number somebody measured.
- Refuse rather than guess. A stage that is not built says so; a claim a
  test cannot hold is not made.
- Keep secrets out of files, repositories and artifacts, and keep real
  people, companies and prices out of examples and tests.
- Respect that the open specifications this product implements belong to
  everyone. Cite them; do not fork or quietly diverge from them here.

Harassment, discrimination and bad-faith conduct are not acceptable in any
channel connected to this work. Concerns go to the accountable contact
named in the estate's flashyos charter, or to the address in `SECURITY.md`
if the concern touches security. Reports are handled privately.
